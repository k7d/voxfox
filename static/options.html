VoxFox

Listen to any webpage with a single click.

How to use

  1. Click the VoxFox icon in the toolbar (pin it from the puzzle-piece menu), or press Alt+Shift+V.
  2. Click again to pause or resume. Use the floating player to skip, change speed, or switch voice.
  3. Select text first to hear only that part, or right-click → Listen to selection.

Voice

Voice model

VoxFox uses Kokoro, an open-weight speech model that runs entirely on your device. Nothing you listen to leaves your computer. The model (~90–330 MB) downloads once from Hugging Face and is cached.
